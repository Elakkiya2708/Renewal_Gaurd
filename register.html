2. Platform Scope & Your Private Workspace

RenewGaurd provides renewal tracking, reminder scheduling and document storage. Every account has its own private workspace: you can only see and manage the renewals and documents you create, and other users cannot access them.

3. Automated Renewal Notifications & Schedules

RenewGaurd dispatches automated alert emails (at customizable intervals such as 90, 60, 30, 14, 7, and 1-day milestones) and in-app notifications. While RenewGaurd maintains continuous dispatch daemons, your organization retains ultimate responsibility for maintaining accurate contract renewal dates, valid recipient email addresses, and taking timely commercial renewal action.

Users must provide accurate, current, and complete registration details. You are responsible for safeguarding your login credentials and preventing unauthorized access to your workspace. Any suspected credential compromise or unauthorized access must be reported immediately.

Privacy Guaranteed Compliant with Modern Privacy Standards

1. Information We Collect

RenewGaurd collects only information necessary to deliver contract renewal tracking and workspace collaboration:

  • Account Profile: Full name, verified business email, contact phone number, and cryptographic password hash.
  • Renewal & Vendor Data: Contract titles, vendor names, renewal dates, cost metrics, billing cycles, and uploaded agreement attachments.
  • Security Audit Logs: Timestamped action records, authenticated IP addresses, and session activities recorded in our tamper-resistant audit trail.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to provide, maintain, and protect your RenewGaurd services:

  • Delivering automated renewal reminders, digest notifications, and email alerts before deadlines.
  • Authenticating your session and keeping your data private to your account.
  • Generating workspace cost forecasts, spend analytics, and compliance audit histories.

3. Security & Cryptographic Protection

We enforce industry-standard security safeguards to protect your confidential corporate data:

  • User passwords are protected using salted scrypt key-derivation algorithms and are never stored in plaintext.
  • Authentication tokens are securely signed with HMAC-SHA256 signatures and session-bound.
  • Data access is strictly isolated by workspace, preventing unauthorized cross-tenant visibility.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Data Selling

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your corporate contract data, user contacts, or renewal records to advertisers or third-party brokers. Data is transmitted solely to operational subprocessors necessary for system notifications.

5. Data Retention & User Rights

You have the right to inspect, export (via CSV/ICS/JSON), update, or delete your data at any time. When an account is deleted, related records and associated reminder queues are purged.
